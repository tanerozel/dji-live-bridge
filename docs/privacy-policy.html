Stream My Drone

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

Introduction

Stream My Drone ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Streaming credentials: RTMP/RTMPS stream URLs and authentication keys you enter to connect to streaming services
  • Device information: Camera feeds from your connected DJI drone
  • Network settings: WiFi connection details for drone communication

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device identifiers: Device model, operating system version, app version
  • Usage analytics: App features used, stream duration, connection attempts
  • Performance data: Video resolution, bitrate, frame rate, stream stability metrics
  • Error logs: Crashes, connection failures, and diagnostic information
  • Network information: WiFi signal strength, connection status

1.3 Sensitive Permissions

The app requests:

  • Camera access: To receive video feed from your drone
  • Microphone access: To capture audio from your device (if available)
  • Network access: To stream video to your configured RTMP destination
  • Local storage: To store temporary streaming data and logs

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Enable video streaming from your drone to your chosen destination
  • Monitor app performance, stability, and reliability
  • Diagnose connection issues and technical problems
  • Improve app features and user experience
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information to:

  • Track your physical location
  • Build advertising profiles
  • Share with third-party advertisers
  • Sell or rent your data

3. RTMP Credentials & Stream Keys

Critical Security Notice:
  • Stream URLs and authentication keys are never sent to our servers
  • They are stored locally on your device only
  • On macOS, credentials are encrypted in the system Keychain
  • On Android, credentials are stored in the app's private secure storage
  • You remain in complete control of all streaming credentials

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • Local-only storage: Most data remains on your device
  • Logs: Diagnostic logs are retained for 7 days, then automatically deleted
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics are retained for 30 days
  • Video data: Camera feeds are not stored; they stream directly to your configured destination
  • No cloud backup: We do not back up your credentials or stream keys to cloud services

5. Third-Party Services

Our app may connect to:

  • Your RTMP server: You configure the destination; we facilitate the connection
  • DJI services: Your drone communicates directly with DJI servers (see DJI's privacy policy)
  • Google Play Services: For app distribution and crash reporting
  • Analytics provider: We use privacy-focused, on-device analytics only

We do not share personal information with these services beyond what is necessary for functionality.

6. Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell your data
  • Share with advertisers or data brokers
  • Disclose stream credentials or streaming destinations to third parties
  • Combine your information with other users' data

We may disclose information if:

  • Required by law or legal process
  • Necessary to protect safety or property
  • Defending against legal claims
  • Enforcing our Terms of Service

7. Children's Privacy

Stream My Drone is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

8. Security Measures

  • Local encryption: Credentials are encrypted at rest on your device
  • In-transit protection: All network communication uses TLS/HTTPS
  • Access control: Only your device can access stored credentials
  • No unnecessary permissions: We request only permissions required for streaming

9. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a summary of data we hold about your app usage
  • Delete: Remove the app to delete all locally stored data immediately
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics in the app settings
  • Control: Manage permissions for camera, microphone, and network access via device settings

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and, if required, by prominent notice in the app.
